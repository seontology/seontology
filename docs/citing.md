# Citing SEOntology

If you use SEOntology, please cite the repository and the SEMANTiCS 2026 paper:

> Emilija Gjorgjevska, David Riccitelli, Milos Jovanovik, Andrea Volpini. *SEOntology: A Domain Ontology for Semantic Modeling of Search Engine Optimization Workflows.* SEMANTiCS 2026, Research Track. Best Student Paper.

```bibtex
@software{gjorgjevska2026seontology,
  title        = {SEOntology: A Domain Ontology for Semantic Modeling of Search Engine Optimization Workflows},
  author       = {Gjorgjevska, Emilija and Riccitelli, David and Jovanovik, Milos and Volpini, Andrea},
  year         = {2026},
  url          = {https://github.com/seontology/seontology},
  note         = {Accepted at SEMANTiCS 2026 Research & Innovation Track}
}
```

Machine-readable citation metadata is in [`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/seontology/seontology/blob/main/CITATION.cff).

## Publications

- Andrea Volpini. "[Introducing SEOntology: The Future Of SEO In The Age Of AI](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/introducing-seontology-the-future-of-seo-in-the-age-of-ai/524773/)". Search Engine Journal, August 2024.
- Emilija Gjorgjevska. "[SEOntology: The Basis for Next-Gen SEO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gM6egAImB1o)". Zagreb SEO Summit, June 2024.

## Evaluation data

The before-and-after evaluation reported in the paper is an aggregated, anonymized overview. The underlying test used client data and internal workflows, so the raw material cannot be released. The paper reports the task context, the comparison the ontology enabled, the observed efficiency and quality improvements, and a summary of team feedback.
